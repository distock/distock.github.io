I've toyed with the idea of starting a blog a dozen times over the last 10 years, but I never actually got around to it. Although I could have shared a lot about my experience with Java, PHP, frontend development, AWS architecture, DevOps, and Kubernetes, I never did.

Times have changed, and I've learned that there is no better way to learn than to write and teach. So, here we go. 

## AI 

As a developer, I am well aware that the impact of AI will change my role or perhaps even make many developers redundant. No one can predict the future, but I want to be ready for it and anticipate as much as I can to stay relevant in the field. That’s why I decided to learn how to work with AI—specifically, how to build AI agents. That journey is what I will log on this blog. No 20-minute posts; just simple, short-form content about the things I learn. 


## Learning

As I mentioned, there is no better way to learn than to write or teach. My goal is to learn a new topic each week and share a short post about it. This is mostly for personal reference, but hopefully, my writing will improve and one day I’ll be able to share something on my favorite news website: [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com). 

## Showcase

I will also document the small projects I build while learning to work with LLMs and AI agents. This will serve as a showcase for clients and potential employers.
